# Project Progress Report - RamBhaji

## 1. Project Overview
Ye project ek aadhunik (modern) aur automated platform hai, jiska main objective customers ko fresh products aur daily needs ka saaman aasani se unke ghar tak pahunchana hai. Is system ke madhyam se users online order kar sakte hain aur daily/weekly subscription le sakte hain. 
Sath hi, Business Owner (Admin) ek aasaan dashboard se apne products, stock, delivery boys aur payments ko ek hi jagah se manage kar sakte hain. Is project ka sabse bada fayda business operations ko fast, transparent aur bilkul error-free banana hai.

## 2. Abhi Tak Kya-Kya Ban Chuka Hai

### Login aur Authentication System
* **Ye kya hai:** Platform me safely enter karne ka secure tareeka.
* **User:** OTP aur password ke madhyam se securely apna account login kar sakta hai.
* **Admin:** Dekh sakta hai ki kitne verified users system me active hain.
* **Business Fayda:** Fake accounts se bachav hota hai aur verified, genuine customers ka data milta hai.

### User Management
* **Ye kya hai:** Customers ke accounts ko manage karne ka hissa.
* **User:** Apni profile, addresses, aur personal details update kar sakta hai.
* **Admin:** Kisi bhi customer ki details dekh sakta hai, aur unki preferences manage kar sakta hai.
* **Business Fayda:** Har customer ka clear record maintain hota hai.

### Product Management
* **Ye kya hai:** Platform par jo bhi items sell ho rahe hain unki list aur settings.
* **User:** Available products ki list, unke price aur details check kar sakta hai.
* **Admin:** Naye products add kar sakta hai, stock aur price update kar sakta hai.
* **Business Fayda:** Customers ko hamesha updated aur available products hi dikhte hain.

### Subscription System
* **Ye kya hai:** Daily ya fix interval (jaise har din ya alternate days) par delivery ka system.
* **User:** Baar-baar order karne ki jhanjhat se bachne ke liye apna daily plan set kar sakta hai.
* **Admin:** Manage kar sakta hai ki aaj kisko kaun sa subscription item bhejna hai.
* **Business Fayda:** Regular aur fix income generate hoti hai, aur roj ke orders ki planning aasaan hoti hai.

### Delivery System
* **Ye kya hai:** Ghar tak saaman pahunchane ka poora track aur process.
* **User:** Apne orders ki delivery status dekh sakta hai.
* **Admin / Delivery Boy:** Delivery boy orders accept kar sakta hai, aur admin poore delivery process ko monitor kar sakta hai.
* **Business Fayda:** Timely delivery ensure hoti hai jisse customer ka bharosa badhta hai.

### Wallet System
* **Ye kya hai:** Digital payment aur balance ka system.
* **User:** Apne wallet me paise daal sakta hai taaki daily orders ke liye automatically payment kat jaye.
* **Admin:** Sabhi users ka balance aur transactions track kar sakta hai.
* **Business Fayda:** Payment lene ki dikkat khatam hoti hai aur cash flow smooth rehta hai.

### Dashboard aur Analytics
* **Ye kya hai:** Poore business ka summary aur health check.
* **Admin:** Ek hi screen par dekh sakta hai ki kitni sale hui, kitne order aaye aur kya profit hua.
* **Business Fayda:** Data dekh kar business ko badhane ke liye sahi decisions liye ja sakte hain.

### Calculator aur Reverse Calculator
* **Ye kya hai:** Hisaab-kitaab karne ka smart tool.
* **Admin:** Products aur quantity ke hisaab se asani se rate aur total calculate kar sakta hai.
* **Business Fayda:** Calculation errors nahi hoti aur manual time bachta hai.

### Batch aur Package Management
* **Ye kya hai:** Alag-alag items ko group karna aur batches me delivery manage karna.
* **User:** Pre-made packages (combos) khareed sakta hai.
* **Admin:** Items ki sorting aur packing ko batches me manage kar sakta hai.
* **Business Fayda:** Packing aur sorting process bahut fast aur organized ho jata hai.


## 3. Admin Panel
Admin panel business owner ke liye ek remote control ki tarah hai. Isme abhi tak ye sab ready hai:
* **Dashboard:** Business ki daily progress aur sales ka summary chart.
* **Product Management:** Saare products aur unki categories ko add ya update karna.
* **User Management:** Sabhi registered customers ka poora data dekhna aur unki queries solve karna.
* **Delivery Management:** Delivery boys ko assign karna aur unki daily performance dekhna.
* **Orders:** Aaj ke naye, pending aur complete ho chuke orders ki list.
* **Reports:** Puraane sales aur profits ka poora hisaab nikalna.
* **Subscription Management:** Daily aane wale recurring orders ka data manage karna.
* **Packages:** Customers ke liye combo offers aur packages create karna.


## 4. User Side
Customer (User) ke liye jo application taiyar hui hai, usme ye features milte hain:
* **Login/Signup:** Secure OTP aur password wali entry.
* **Dashboard:** User ke apne orders aur wallet balance ka summary.
* **Address Management:** Multiple delivery addresses save karne ka option.
* **Orders:** Apne purane aur naye orders ki details check karna.
* **Wallet:** Paise add karna aur purani transactions ki history dekhna.
* **Subscription:** Naye plans lagana aur purane plans ko rokna ya change karna.
* **Retail Store:** Normal products ko seedhe add-to-cart karke order karna.


## 5. Delivery Module
Delivery staff aur delivery process ke liye ye module banaya gaya hai:
* **Delivery Person View:** Delivery boy apne app me dekh sakta hai ki aaj use kahan-kahan jana hai aur kaun sa order deliver karna hai.
* **Delivery Process:** Jab boy nikalta hai toh status update hota hai, aur deliver hone par complete mark ho jata hai.
* **Delivery Proof:** Order deliver karte samay proof (photo ya OTP) liya ja sakta hai taaki koi customer ye na kahe ki order nahi mila.


## 6. Authentication
* **Secure Login:** System me entry bilkul safe hai.
* **OTP Verification:** Taaki koi fake number se register na kar sake; asli number confirm hota hai.
* **Password Reset:** Agar koi apna password bhool jaye, toh easily OTP ke madhyam se naya password bana sakta hai.
* **Role wise access:** Admin, User aur Delivery Boy - teeno ko sirf wahi cheezein dikhti hain jo unke kaam ki hain. Data bilkul safe rehta hai.
